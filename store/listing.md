# Google Play listing: NEPSE Lens

**Title (≤30):** NEPSE Lens: Nepal Stock Market
**Short description (≤80):** NEPSE live board, broker accumulation, alerts & portfolio P/L in Nepali/English

## Full description
NEPSE Lens is a fast, clean Nepal Stock Exchange (NEPSE) app built for your phone.

• Market board: NEPSE index with trend line, market open/closed countdown, turnover, top gainers, losers and most active, and a sector heatmap.
• Stock pages: price chart with 20-day average and volume, 52-week range, RSI and returns.
• Broker accumulation: see which brokers have been net buying or selling a stock over 1 day, 1 week, 1 month, 3 months or since tracking began. Computed from NEPSE floorsheets. (NEPSE does not publish broker holdings; this is net buying, not holdings.)
• Brokers: each broker's top net buys and sells.
• Watchlist and price alerts with notifications.
• Portfolio: WACC, profit/loss after broker commission, SEBON fee, DP charge and capital gains tax.
• Fee calculator and break-even price.
• IPO, FPO and right-share calendar.
• Lakh/crore formatting, English and Nepali, dark trading-terminal design, works offline with the last data.

Data is delayed and unofficial, collected from public NEPSE web data. NEPSE Lens is not affiliated with NEPSE, SEBON or any broker. Nothing in the app is investment advice.
